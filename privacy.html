TAPHOUSE APP – PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 1st February 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TapHouse App (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use the TapHouse App mobile application (“App”). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

  1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data when you use TapHouse App:

1.1 User Profile Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • User ID
  • Role (admin/user)
  • Date of account creation
  • Date of last account update

1.2 User Preferences & Activity

  • Terms of Use acceptance version and timestamp
  • List of favourited pub IDs
  • List of visited pub IDs
  • Onboarding completion status
  • Selected amenity preferences

1.3 Review Data

  • Pub ID and name being reviewed
  • User ID, name, and email associated with the review
  • Rating (1–5 stars)
  • Review comment

1.4 Feedback Data

  • Pub ID and name the feedback relates to
  • Feedback type
  • Feedback message
  • User email (if provided)

1.5 Pub Claim Requests

  • User email requesting to claim a pub
  • Information submitted as part of the claim verification process

1.6 Automatically Collected Data (if applicable)

Depending on your device and settings, we may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • App usage analytics
  • Crash logs
  1. 2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 To Operate and Provide the App

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Saving preferences, favourites, and visited pubs
  • Displaying personalised content
  • Processing reviews, feedback, and pub claim requests

2.2 To Improve the App

  • Understanding feature usage
  • Fixing bugs and improving performance
  • Enhancing user experience

2.3 To Maintain Safety and Prevent Abuse

  • Detecting suspicious activity
  • Preventing scraping or misuse of App data
  • Verifying pub ownership claims

2.4 Legal Compliance

  • Maintaining Terms of Use acceptance logs
  • Responding to lawful requests
  1. 3. Legal Bases for Processing (UK GDPR)

We process your data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contract: To provide the App and its features.
  • Legitimate Interests: Improving the App, preventing abuse, analytics, and maintaining security.
  • Consent: When you voluntarily submit optional information (e.g., feedback email).
  1. 4. How We Share Your Information

4.1 Service Providers

We may share your data with service providers such as:

  • Hosting providers
  • Analytics providers
  • Email delivery services

These providers only process data on our behalf and under strict confidentiality.

4.2 Third-Party APIs

If the App interacts with external services (for example, venue data providers), only the necessary information is transmitted.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our rights. We do not sell your personal data.

  1. 5. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes described:

  • User account data: Kept until you delete your account.
  • Reviews: Kept unless you request deletion.
  • Feedback submissions: Kept for service improvement.
  • Terms acceptance logs: Kept indefinitely for legal compliance.
  • Pub claim requests: Kept for verification and audit purposes.
  1. 6. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data.
  • Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data.
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing.
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

  1. 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  1. 8. Children’s Privacy

TapHouse App is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

  1. 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
